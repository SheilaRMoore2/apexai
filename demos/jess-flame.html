The maker

It started with one pot on a kitchen stove

Jess started blending scents because nothing on the shelf smelled like the house she grew up in. The first batch was twelve jars for family. The recipe hasn't changed much since — it just gets poured a little more often now.

Everything is still made in-house: blended, poured, labeled, and boxed by hand before it goes out. One person, one studio.

Good to know

Questions, answered

What are the eight scents?

Lavender Fields, Warm Vanilla Bean, Citrus Grove, Fresh Linen, Amber & Oud, and Mango & Coconut Milk — plus two more scents in the collection (placeholders in this demo for the shop to confirm).

How long do the candles burn?

About 45–50 hours on average. Trim the wick before lighting, let the wax melt to the edges on the first burn, and you'll get the full, true scent to the last hour.

What is the wax, and why the two-week cure?

Coconut–soy wax with cotton wicks, for a clean burn. The two-week cure lets the scent bind to the wax, so it stays true from the first burn to the last instead of fading halfway down the jar.

Do you ship? Is shipping free?

Yes — free US shipping on orders over $100. Shipping options and delivery times beyond that are placeholders in this concept demo, to be confirmed by Jess Flame before launch.

What else do you sell besides candles?

A travel tin, a room and body mist, and a melt sampler — same wax, same wicks, same two-week cure. Ask Ember about any of them.
