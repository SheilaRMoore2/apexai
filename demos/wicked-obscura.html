How a ritual candle works

Set it. Light it. Let it go.

A Wicked Obscura candle is a tool, not just a scent. The fragrance sets the mood, the crystal holds the intention, and the flame marks the moment.

1

Choose your intention

Release, focus, love, clarity — or simply a quiet room. Nyx can match a candle to it.

2

Light with purpose

Trim the wick, take a breath, and name what you're calling in or letting go of.

3

Keep the crystal

When the wax is gone, the crystal topping remains — a keepsake of the ritual.

Good to know

Questions, answered

What do the crystals on top mean?

Each crystal is chosen for the candle's intention: iolite for vision and trust in the unknown, rainbow moonstone for balance and intuition, amethyst for clarity and spiritual connection, rose quartz for love and harmony. Ask Nyx about any candle's stone.

Do you have unscented options?

Yes — Ghost is an unscented soy candle topped with rainbow moonstone, made for meditation, rituals, and scent-sensitive spaces where clarity and stillness matter more than fragrance.

How do shipping and delivery work?

Shipping options and delivery times are placeholders in this concept demo, to be confirmed by Wicked Obscura before launch. On the live version, Nyx would confirm your ZIP and give exact options here.

Can I burn a ritual candle like a normal candle?

Yes — with the usual care: trim the wick, burn on a heat-safe surface, keep it away from drafts, and never leave it unattended. Keep crystal and botanical toppings away from the flame as the wax melts.

What else do you sell besides candles?

The Witchy Nook: enamel pins (like the Ghost Hunter Club pin), tarot mugs, hoodies, phone cases, and puzzles — many made to order. Ask Nyx about any of them.
